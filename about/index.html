About Bhoomi Brass Products

A Jamnagar brass components manufacturer for business buyers seeking product-range or made-to-drawing manufacturing discussions.

Jamnagar · Gujarat · India

Focused on brass components and buyer requirements

Bhoomi Brass Products is based in Jamnagar, Gujarat, India. The stated product range includes brass inserts, brass pressure parts, brass terminals and custom brass components made to drawing. We welcome enquiries from manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors and other B2B buyers who can share a defined component requirement.

A drawing, sample or written specification gives both sides a practical starting point. For a useful technical review, include the component’s intended use, material grade, dimensions, thread details, critical tolerances, finish, quantity and delivery destination. If a detail is not yet fixed, identify it as open for discussion rather than assuming a standard.

Business History and Operating Scale Our company was established in 1993 and has developed its manufacturing capabilities over the years.
